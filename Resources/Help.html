Easy Img Help

A fast, keyboard-driven image viewer: open an image and flip through its whole folder with the arrow keys.

Opening images and folders

  • From Finder: right-click an image → Open With → Easy Img. For images and folders, right-click → Quick Actions (or Services) → Open in Easy Img.
  • Drag images or folders onto the Easy Img icon in the Dock.
  • Inside the app: File → Open… (⌘O), or pick a folder in the sidebar.
  • To make Easy Img the default viewer: select an image in Finder → Get Info → Open with: Easy Img → Change All…

Opening a single image shows it with the rest of its folder ready to browse. Opening a folder shows its images as thumbnails. Opening several selected items browses just those.

Browsing

  • ← / → go to the previous / next image. At the end of the folder it wraps around to the start.
  • Home / End jump to the first / last image.
  • The window title shows the file name, its position (for example 12 of 172) and the zoom level.
  • Sidebar: the button at the top left (⌃⌘S) shows a folder tree. Click a folder to see its images.
  • Filmstrip: the strip of thumbnails under the image. Click one to jump to it. It can be turned off in Settings → General or View → Show Filmstrip.

Thumbnail grid

  • Press G (or the grid button in the toolbar) to switch between the image and the grid.
  • Use the arrow keys to move the selection. Return, Space or a double-click opens the image.
  • In the image view, Esc goes back to the grid with that image selected.
  • + / − make the thumbnails bigger or smaller.
  • Thumbnails show the whole picture. To crop them into even squares, turn on Settings → General → Crop thumbnails or View → Crop Thumbnails to Squares.

Zoom and scrolling

  • + / − zoom in and out, 0 fits the image to the window, 1 shows it at actual size. You can also pinch on the trackpad.
  • Each image opens fitted to the window, and small images are never enlarged.
  • When the image is bigger than the window, scroll with the trackpad or wheel, or drag the image.
  • A minimap appears in the bottom-right corner, with a frame showing the part you're looking at. Click or drag inside it to move around.
  • Large photos first load at screen resolution, then at full resolution when you zoom in far enough.

Ask AI

Ask questions about the image you're viewing: what it shows, the text it contains, a translation, where a photo might have been taken, and so on.

Setting up

  1. Open Easy Img → Settings… (⌘,) → AI.
  2. Choose the Assistant provider: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi or Custom.
  3. Paste that provider's API key. Keys are stored in your macOS Keychain.
  4. Optionally press Load Models and choose another model.

Custom works with any OpenAI-compatible server, such as OpenRouter, or Ollama and LM Studio on your Mac. Enter its address (for Ollama, http://localhost:11434/v1), type the model name in Model, and tick The custom server's model can see images if it can.

Chatting

  • Press ⌘K or the ✨ button to open the chat panel on the right. Type a question and press Return.
  • The chat follows the image you're viewing. Each image keeps its own conversation while the window is open, and the pencil button starts a new one.
  • Each answer shows which model replied and its estimated cost. The stop button ends an answer early.
  • To switch models quickly, turn on Show a model picker in the chat panel in Settings → AI.

Privacy

An image is sent to the AI provider only when you ask a question about it, resized to at most 1568 pixels. Models that can't see images (such as deepseek-v4-pro) receive an on-device description instead: the objects and text macOS recognized in the image. In that case the image itself never leaves your Mac.

Costs and spending limit

  • Settings → Costs shows spending today and this month, a breakdown by model, and recent requests with the tokens they used.
  • Monthly spending limit: tick it and enter an amount in US dollars. The chat warns when you pass 80%. Once the limit is reached, each request asks first, and you can Allow This Request.
  • Prices: costs are estimates: the tokens each provider reports multiplied by the prices in the table. Prices change, so you can edit them, add models with +, or press Reset to Defaults. A model matches the longest entry its name starts with.
  • Clear History… erases the recorded requests, which also resets this month's total.

Your provider's bill is the final word. Requests stopped before they finish may not be counted.

Troubleshooting

  • "Easy Img wants to use your confidential information stored in…": this is macOS asking to read the saved API key from your Keychain. Choose Always Allow. It can ask again after the app is rebuilt or updated.
  • "Easy Img would like to access files in your Documents/Downloads folder": macOS asks the first time you browse a protected folder. Allow it to see that folder's images.
  • A thumbnail shows a generic icon: macOS couldn't make a preview of that file. It may be damaged or in an unsupported format.
  • The chat says there is no API key: add one in Settings → AI for the provider selected as Assistant.
  • An AI error mentions the model: the model name may be wrong or unavailable to your account. Press Load Models in Settings → AI and choose one from the list.
